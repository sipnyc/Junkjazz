    Junk: personnel

    Formed in 2024. All five met on the bandstand at the Sunday residency.

    Michael Junkins

    Guitar, leader

    Had the idea for this band for years before the right four players walked into the Den.

    Will Hudgins

    Vibraphone, composer

    A Boston Symphony Orchestra percussionist for more than 30 years. Writes most of the band's book, including "Mouse House." Also plays with the Ron Bosse Group.

    About Michael

    Michael Junkins heard John Coltrane's "My Favorite Things" at sixteen and never got over it. He picked jazz guitar and spent a decade living and working in Philadelphia, where he played with and learned from guitarists including Steve Giordano, Jimmy Bruno and Pat Martino. He credits those years for the personal style he plays in now.

    Since moving to the Berkshires he has performed across the Northeast for more than two decades. In 2019 he pitched the Red Lion Inn on turning the Lion's Den into a jazz room. It is now the steadiest jazz gig in the county, with a rotating cast and an open door for visiting players who want to sit in.

    He books local musicians first, on purpose. The goal is a scene that can sustain itself, not just a gig.

    "We all bring our passion to it."

    Michael Junkins, to BTW Berkshires
